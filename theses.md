## PAXG_USDT — opened 2026-08-20 15:01Z

- **position:** 0.014395108845 units · blended cost basis **4512.75** · ~65 USDT (**44% of book**)
  - leg 1: 0.00776518 @ 4507.32 (35 USDT)
  - leg 2: 0.00663713 @ 4520.02 (30 USDT) — the cash-weight correction below
- **thesis — fiscal dominance:** the market's organising story today is not the war, it is the
  US fiscal position. Public debt crossed **$40 trillion** for the first time. The **30-year
  yield hit 5.31% on Monday, the highest since 2007**. The Treasury responded by **doubling its
  long-bond buyback operations, from $2bn to a minimum of $4bn per operation, beginning
  9 September**; the 30y fell to 5.19% and the 10y to 4.66% on the announcement. The Treasury is
  now the marginal buyer of its own long end. That is the regime in which hard assets re-rate.
- **why it is not already priced:** gold moved +4% and silver +6.4% on 08-19, so one day is
  traded. **But the buyback operations do not begin until 9 September** — the catalyst has been
  *announced*, not *executed*, and the flow is entirely ahead. The $40tn milestone is structural.
- **why PAXG rather than XAUT or a gold perp:** physically-backed, genuine 24/7 price discovery,
  no equity-issuer layer, NYDFS-regulated trust, and 8× XAUT's liquidity on Pionex (1.88M vs
  232k 24h volume). For an agent that does not exist between runs and cannot rest a stop, an
  instrument that prices continuously matters more than thematic precision.
- **falsifiers:**
  1. the Treasury cancels, shrinks, or quietly under-executes the 9 September programme
  2. the 30y falls back below ~4.75% on *genuine* demand rather than official support — the
     fiscal-stress premise dissolves
  3. gold closes below **~4,250**, breaking the trend the announcement started
  4. a September hike lands **and** gold sells off with it — that would mean the front end is
     winning and the regime read is simply wrong
- **known risk, taken deliberately:** fed funds futures price **~82% odds of a hike on
  16 September**, up from ~53% a week earlier, driven by the same oil shock this book is long. A
  hike raises the carrying cost of a non-yielding metal. Taking the other side because those odds
  rose to 82% over the same week metals rallied — the market is pricing the split, front end
  hikes while the long end gets monetised.
- **horizon:** weeks to months. Unlike USOX this is a regime trade, not a headline trade.
- **why it is the largest position — the cash-weight correction:** the run-3 plan left 35% of the
  book in USDT and justified it as ammunition. Erik challenged it and the challenge was right.
  **Holding a third of the book in dollars while the book's central thesis is that the US is
  monetising its own long end is a position against that thesis** — cash is not neutral in a
  debasement trade, it is the short side of it. The ammunition argument is separately weak here:
  PAXG turns over ~1.88M USDT a day and this is unleveraged spot, so any position is one market
  order from being cash again. Optionality repurchasable for a few basis points is not worth 35%
  of the book. Added 30 USDT; cash to 22.07 (14.8%), which is two Pionex minimum orders — a
  functional floor so the next run can act without a forced sale, not a reserve held out of nerves.

  obsolete. Nothing in this book is a perp; every position above is unleveraged spot and
  therefore cannot be liquidated.
- **Verify fills with `orders fills`, not `orders get`.** On run 3 `orders get` returned
  `TRADE_ORDER_NOT_EXIST` twice for a SLVX market order that had in fact filled. Never conclude
  an order failed from a not-found lookup — check `orders fills` and the balance first.
  `orders get` also requires `--symbol` alongside `--order-id`.
